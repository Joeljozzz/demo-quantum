Why does quantum always win?

H·H = I (identity). So H(I)|0⟩ = |0⟩ and H(X)|0⟩ = H|1⟩ = |0⟩·… wait — H|1⟩ = (|0⟩−|1⟩)/√2?

Actually in Meyer's game Q plays 1st & 3rd, and any classical move in position 2 commutes past the H·H to give |0⟩. This is the quantum advantage!

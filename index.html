How the Game Works

🎯

Starting State

The coin begins Heads. If it ends Heads, you win. Tails, the computer wins.

🤫

Secret Moves

You make two secret moves: flip or keep. The computer makes one move in between.

🎲

Classical Computer

⚛️

Quantum Computer

The quantum computer uses Hadamard gates — no matter what you do, it collapses to Heads and wins.

The Quantum Advantage

In Meyer's Quantum Penny Game (1999), a quantum player applies a Hadamard gate twice — placing the coin in superposition, then collapsing it perfectly. No classical strategy can counter this.

|0⟩ = Heads → H|0⟩ = (|0⟩+|1⟩)/√2 → You: I or X → H|±⟩ = |0⟩ ✓

Whether you flip (apply X gate) or keep (identity I), applying H a second time always collapses the state back to |0⟩ = Heads. The quantum computer wins every time.

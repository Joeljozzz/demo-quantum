How the Game Works

Goal

Starting State

The coin begins Heads. If it ends Heads, you win. Tails, the computer wins.

Secret

Secret Moves

You make two secret moves: flip or keep. The computer makes one move in between.

Random

Classical Computer

The classical computer picks randomly — giving you a genuine 50/50 chance to win.

Quantum

Quantum Computer

The quantum computer uses Hadamard gates — no matter what you do, it collapses to Heads and wins.

The Quantum Advantage

A quantum player applies a Hadamard gate twice — placing the coin in superposition, then collapsing it perfectly. No classical strategy can counter this.
